Lesson One
§ 01 / RULES

The
Laws.

Football's rulebook is short by design. Eleven players a side, ninety minutes split in two, and a ball that must — without exception — fully cross the line for things to count. The rest is etiquette.

01

The Match

Two teams of eleven, including a goalkeeper. Two halves of 45 minutes plus injury time. Whoever scores more, wins.

FUNDAMENTAL
02

The Goal

The whole ball must cross the whole line, between the posts and under the bar. No part remaining on the line — not by a hair.

DECISIVE
03

Offside

An attacker is offside if they're closer to the goal than both the ball and the second-to-last defender at the moment a teammate plays the ball forward to them.

CONTESTED
04

Hands

Outfield players cannot deliberately touch the ball with hand or arm. Only the goalkeeper, inside their own penalty area, may use hands.

FOUL
05

The Cards

Yellow is a warning. Red is a sending-off — the team plays on with ten. Two yellows in one match equals a red.

DISCIPLINE
06

Free Kicks

For most fouls play stops and the wronged team kicks from where it happened. Direct kicks can score; indirect ones must touch a second player first.

RESTART
07

Penalty Kicks

A foul inside your own penalty area is a penalty: a direct shot from twelve yards, only the keeper to beat. The hardest goal to miss, the easiest to bottle.

HIGH STAKES
08

Throw-Ins

When the ball leaves the touchline, the team that didn't touch it last throws it back in — both feet on the ground, ball over the head with two hands.

RESTART
09

Substitutions

Most competitions allow five substitutions, used in three windows. Once off, you don't come back on.

§ 04 / TECHNIQUE

The
Tools.

You don't need to be Messi. You do need a first touch that doesn't betray you, a pass that finds a friend, and a shot that goes where you mean it. Six basics that translate everywhere.

★ ☆ ☆ ☆ ☆ — Beginner

First Touch

The single most important skill in the game. Your first contact decides whether the next move is forward or panicked.

Move toward the ball Soften your foot at impact Direct it into space, not your feet
★ ★ ☆ ☆ ☆ — Easy

The Pass

Inside of the foot, ankle locked, follow through. Aim for the player your teammate is becoming, not the player they are.

Plant foot points to target Strike with the side of the foot Pass into stride, not feet
★ ★ ★ ☆ ☆ — Intermediate

The Shot

Pace and placement, not power. Aim for the corners — keepers can save anything within reach, but they can't reach what's tucked low and far.

Approach from a slight angle Plant foot beside the ball Strike with laces, knee over the ball
★ ★ ★ ☆ ☆ — Intermediate

Dribbling

Small touches, head up. The ball stays inside an imaginary box around your feet — never far enough that an opponent can poke it away.

Use both feet, both insides Touches every step, not every yard Head up — see the field, not the ball
★ ★ ★ ★ ☆ — Advanced

The Header

Use your forehead, not the top of your head. Attack the ball — don't let the ball attack you. Eyes open, neck strong.

Time your jump, not the ball's flight Strike with the forehead Aim downward — keepers struggle with low
★ ★ ★ ★ ★ — Advanced

The Feint

A lie told with the body. Lean one way, push the ball the other. A defender's weight has to commit somewhere — make them commit wrong.

Drop the shoulder one way Push the ball the opposite way Accelerate — don't admire
